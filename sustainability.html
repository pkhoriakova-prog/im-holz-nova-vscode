Our approach

Our Approach

Sustainability that can be measured

For us, sustainable development is a set of results that can be checked, not a slogan. No single technology delivers them alone, so we combine materials, engineering, energy, water and local expertise in one process, aligned with EN 15978, the EU Taxonomy and the UN SDGs.

  1. Lower embodied carbon

    Certified engineered timber, with life-cycle data that makes the saving visible.

  2. Healthier indoor spaces

    Natural materials, daylight and good air for the people who use the building every day.

  3. Resource-efficient construction

    Prefabricated, dry-assembled elements: less waste on site and a shorter build.

  4. Buildings that last

    Designed for a 50-year service life and for uses that stay relevant to their communities.

Announcement

A House of Austrian Technologies for Vienna is taking shape

We are developing the concept for a demonstration centre in Vienna exploring Austrian approaches to sustainable development. Conceived as both a working space and a living showcase, it will bring together materials, technologies and expertise across sectors — demonstrating how proven solutions can work together in real-world environments.

The project is currently at an early stage, with its scope, partnerships and funding model being defined. Our ambition is to create more than a showroom: a place where technologies can be experienced in context, ideas can be tested and new collaborations can take shape — in Vienna and beyond.

Eco Solutions

Nine solutions, one way of building

The technologies behind our projects, each explained on its own page: how it is applied, what it delivers and where it has been proven. Pages are added as the material is ready.

  • 01

    Engineered timber

    CLT and glulam as the load-bearing structure. Panels and frames made from certified timber, prefabricated to precise tolerances and assembled quickly on site.

    Read more

  • 02

    Prefabricated envelope

    Facade elements assembled dry and delivered in a fixed sequence. Less work on site, a tighter building envelope and a predictable schedule.

  • 03

    Hybrid construction

    Concrete kept to where it earns its place: cores, stairs and foundations. Timber carries the rest, which lowers embodied carbon without compromising stability.

  • 04

    Fire safety

    Austrian know-how in timber design and detailing. Protective layers and tested connections keep timber buildings safe.

  • 05

    Green roofs

    Planting carried on the timber deck itself. The roof retains rainwater, cools the building in summer and adds habitat above the city.

  • 06

    Energy & heating

    Renewable energy for low-carbon operation. Efficient heating and solar generation matched to the building and its climate.

  • 07

    Water & sanitation

    Treatment and reuse for public buildings. Clean water supply and sanitation, adapted to local conditions.

  • 08

    Indoor climate

    Ventilation and daylight for the people inside. Natural materials and good air quality support health, comfort and concentration.

  • 09

    Circular construction

    Dry connections: members bolted rather than cast in, so the frame can come apart and be reused at the end of its life.
