Sustainability through integrated solutions

Sustainability depends on how solutions work together in a specific place. We help connect project needs with building, energy, water and resource-efficient technologies, drawing on Austrian and international expertise and local knowledge.

Our approach

Our Approach

Building better, together

We start with the need and explore how materials, energy, water, indoor conditions and long-term operation could work together. Timber is one option among others. Specialist partners assess performance and feasibility; our role is to connect their input and support a shared project direction.

  1. Lower embodied carbon

    Material choices, energy systems and reuse strategies can help reduce impacts. Project-specific life-cycle assessment is needed to compare options.

  2. Healthier indoor spaces

    Natural materials, daylight and good air for the people who use the building every day.

  3. Resource-efficient construction

    Efficient use of materials, energy and water, with prefabrication and reuse considered where suitable.

  4. Buildings that stay in use

    Planning schools, healthcare and public buildings for uses that can stay relevant to their communities.

Announcement

A House of Austrian Technologies for Vienna is taking shape

We are developing the concept for a demonstration centre in Vienna exploring Austrian approaches to sustainable development. Conceived as both a working space and a living showcase, it is meant to bring together materials, technologies and expertise across sectors — showing how Austrian solutions can work together in real-world environments.

The project is currently at an early stage, with its scope, partnerships and funding model being defined. Our ambition is to create more than a showroom: a place where technologies can be experienced in context, ideas can be tested and new collaborations can take shape — in Vienna and beyond.

Eco Solutions

Technology options to match the brief

Options are grouped by the task they help solve: construction, forestry and ecology, energy, water and climate, and research and materials. They are options to assess with specialist partners, rather than a fixed package. Where a detail page is ready, you can open it from the list.

  1. Timber, modular and combined building systems, with natural and low-carbon materials, for schools, housing, public buildings and tourism infrastructure.

    • Engineered timber: CLT and glulam
    • Modular and prefabricated buildings
    • Hybrid and combined construction systems
    • Natural and low-carbon materials
    • Fire safety
    • Circular construction

  2. Restoring and managing forests, and greening territories, with species chosen for local climate conditions.

    • Reforestation
    • Forest nurseries
    • Choice of tree species for local climate
    • Management of forest resources
    • Digital monitoring of forests and ecosystems
    • Landscaping and greening

  3. Complementary solutions that make an infrastructure project more efficient and better adapted to its climate.

    • Energy-efficient buildings
    • Renewable energy sources
    • Water-saving systems
    • Water treatment and reuse
    • Protection from heat and other climate impacts

  4. Testing and studying timber and new materials so that choices rest on evidence for the local climate and use.

    • Timber testing
    • Valuable and little-used tree species
    • Strength and durability assessment
    • New timber processing technologies
    • Materials in different climates

Standards & Certifications

Evidence and standards for selected solutions

Evidence requirements depend on technology, location and project. These examples include product certification, environmental assessment and financing criteria. Relevant specialists establish applicability and carry out required assessments; these are not company certifications.

FSC / PEFC

Certified timber

Forest certification schemes that confirm timber comes from responsibly managed forests.

CE marking

CE-marked products

The EU marking for construction products placed on the European market.

EN 15978

Life-cycle assessment

The European standard for assessing the environmental performance of buildings over their life cycle.

EU Taxonomy

Green finance criteria

The EU classification of environmentally sustainable activities, used to screen green finance.
