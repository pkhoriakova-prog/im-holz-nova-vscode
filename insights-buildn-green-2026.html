Circularity in action

We were at BUILD’N GREEN 2026 in Vienna, this year under the title “Circularity in Action”, to meet people who are shaping more sustainable ways to build.

The two-day conference took place on 28 and 29 April at the Austrian Federal Economic Chamber in Vienna and online. It combined talks, panels and workshops with business matchmaking and excursions, and followed the whole life of a building: resources and materials, planning and financing, use and operation, and deconstruction, recycling and reuse. A special focus lay on rebuilding Ukraine.

Circularity is close to how we think about timber: a renewable material that holds on to the carbon it has absorbed for as long as it stays in use. It was a good place to meet partners who share that view.
