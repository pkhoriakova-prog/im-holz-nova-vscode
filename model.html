Integrator Model

The same project, with fewer missteps

Producers have reached distant markets before. Governments have found European partners before. A dedicated integrator, who holds all the relationships at once and has no competing interest in any single part of the chain, makes it happen faster, with fewer missteps and with better outcomes for everyone. We do not build and we do not trade.

Financing Approach

Blended finance, shaped
for IFI instruments

Projects in emerging markets rarely run on a single source of money. We help structure the financing and prepare each project the way international financial institutions assess it.

  • Blended finance

    Public, concessional and private capital combined in one structure, so each source does the part it is best placed for.

  • IFI instruments

    Projects are shaped around the instruments development finance institutions offer, and around their tender and procurement requirements.

  • Ready for lenders

    Life-cycle carbon data (EN 15978), EU Taxonomy screening and green-building certification, so the technical case is in place before the financing conversation starts.

  • Documented supply

    Certified timber (FSC/PEFC) and CE-marked components with documented origin, so publicly funded projects meet procurement and audit requirements.

Service Cycle

From first brief to handover

Five stages, and we stay with a project through all of them.

  1. We understand the need, the site and the people who will use the building.

  2. Manufacturers, engineers, authorities and local teams are brought into one conversation.

  3. The concept is shaped and the financing route structured side by side, so neither waits for the other.

  4. The design is detailed and matched with certified European products and systems.

  5. Expertise is directed to site, local teams are supported and the building is handed over.
