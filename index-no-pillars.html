About the IM HOLZ Model

The Shift

From timber trade to integrated timber infrastructure

Wood has always been at the core of what we do — a renewable material that connects the built environment with nature. After two decades of trading timber across international markets, we have moved beyond supplying material to understanding what it can enable: better infrastructure that is resource-efficient, adaptable and designed to work with nature.

For Whom

Adopting a new way of building is a risk. We make it a decision you can defend. New markets and new materials carry risk. We take out the parts that make them hard to invest in. We take your systems to markets you would not enter alone — on open terms, with no single preferred supplier.

  • See it before you decide

    Our demonstration centre in Vienna (in development) will let delegations see Austrian timber construction working alongside energy and water solutions — and take home a concrete reference for their own programmes.

  • Proven systems, local skills

    Engineered timber has been standard in Austrian schools and public buildings for more than two decades. Programmes build on that track record, and local teams are trained in assembly and maintenance, so the expertise stays in the country.

  • Prepared for financing

    We prepare projects the way international lenders assess them: life-cycle carbon data (EN 15978), EU Taxonomy screening and green-building certification — so the technical case is in place before the financing conversation starts.

  • Traceable from forest to site

    Certified timber (FSC/PEFC) and CE-marked components with documented origin, so publicly funded projects meet procurement and audit requirements.

Request a project brief

  • Assets that hold their value

    Designed for the same 50-year service life as any building under Eurocode, and documented to EU Taxonomy criteria, so the asset can qualify for green bonds and sustainability-linked finance.

  • Ahead of regulation

    Whole-life carbon disclosure becomes mandatory for new buildings in the EU from 2028/2030. Projects built to that standard today avoid costly retrofits and reporting gaps later.

  • One point of coordination

    Technology providers, engineering, financing and public partners are brought together through IM HOLZ Nova — one counterpart instead of a dozen separate negotiations, in a format designed to be repeated across a wider programme.

  • Supply already in place

    IM HOLZ Group has traded timber internationally for more than twenty years, with a European supplier base. Project sourcing builds on that track record.

  • Open to every manufacturer

    We are not tied to a single supplier. Each project is matched with the systems that fit it best, so any qualified European manufacturer of CLT, glulam or timber building systems can take part. Terms are agreed project by project. We do not manufacture — we don't compete with you.

  • New markets, without a local office

    We help with the groundwork in emerging markets — regulatory approval, public procurement and IFI tender requirements — so manufacturers can supply projects there without building a local presence first.

  • Prepared projects, not cold leads

    Our aim is that projects reach you with a defined brief, a financing route and local partners identified — so you quote on real opportunities, not early enquiries.

  • Visibility with decision-makers

    Our planned demonstration centre in Vienna will present manufacturers' systems directly to visiting delegations, ministries and investors.
