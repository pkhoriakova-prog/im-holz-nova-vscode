02 / Website

Delivery and security

Data and purposes
Accessing this website involves processing technical connection data, including your IP address, request time, requested address, transfer volume, HTTP status and browser-supplied information about your browser, operating system and, where provided, referring page. This enables delivery of the website, troubleshooting and prevention of misuse.
Legal basis
Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is the secure and reliable operation of this website. Connection data required to deliver the website is technically necessary; without it the website cannot be provided.
Recipients and storage
Technical data may be processed by providers engaged for hosting, infrastructure and maintenance. Logs are needed only for as long as required for troubleshooting, operational security or investigation of specific security incidents. Relevant incident data may be retained until the investigation is complete and for the protection of legal claims.

03 / Contact

Email and telephone enquiries

Data and purposes
When you contact us, we process the contact details, message content, any attachments and technical communication data you provide to deal with your enquiry and communicate with you. The website provides email and telephone links; selecting an email link opens your own email application.
Legal basis
Article 6(1)(b) GDPR applies where your enquiry concerns a contract or steps taken at your request before entering into a contract. Other enquiries are handled under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR; our legitimate interest is responding appropriately to communications addressed to us.
Recipients and duration
Access is limited to relevant personnel within our company and, where necessary for technical delivery, our communication and IT providers. Enquiries are retained until they and any necessary follow-up have been dealt with. Further retention is limited to applicable legal obligations or the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims. The duration depends on the content, contractual context and applicable statutory retention and limitation periods.
Voluntary information
Contacting us is voluntary. Without suitable contact details or sufficient information about your request, we may be unable to respond. Please do not send sensitive information that is unnecessary for your enquiry.

04 / Browser

Cookies, fonts and analytics

Public pages
In its current configuration, the public website does not use analytics or advertising cookies and does not create visitor profiles for advertising. Google Analytics, advertising pixels and similar tracking services are not integrated.
Language selection
The language is determined by the page address. Links switch between German and English without setting a persistent language cookie. You can delete language cookies stored before this change using your browser settings.
Local resources
The Manrope font and website images are delivered by our own web server. The font does not require a connection to Google Fonts. WordPress emoji detection that uses browser storage is disabled on public pages.
Administration
Authorised users signing in to WordPress administration may require technically necessary authentication and security cookies. This is separate from browsing the publicly available pages.

06 / Your rights

Access and control

Your rights
Subject to the applicable conditions, you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and data portability. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it at any time with future effect; this does not affect the lawfulness of earlier processing.
Right to object
You may object at any time, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to processing based on Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. We will stop processing the data concerned unless we demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds or need it for the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims.
Contact and response
Please send requests to austria@imholz-group.com. We normally respond within one month and will inform you of any extension permitted by law. We may request proportionate additional information to verify your identity and prevent disclosure to an unauthorised person.
Complaints
You may lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, particularly in the member state of your habitual residence, workplace or the alleged infringement. In Austria: Österreichische Datenschutzbehörde, Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Vienna; website: https://www.dsb.gv.at/.
Automated decisions
This website does not involve automated decision-making, including profiling, producing legal or similarly significant effects.
