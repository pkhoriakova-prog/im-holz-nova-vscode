About the IM HOLZ Model

The Shift

From timber trade to sustainable solutions integration

Timber and natural materials remain our foundation. Over two decades in international timber markets we have come to know the whole chain, from raw material and production to certified, finished buildings, and built relationships with manufacturers and partners around the world.

Sustainable solutions integration is the next logical step on that path, not a change of direction. Today we go further: we connect sustainable construction with energy, water and resource-efficient solutions, together with the right partners and specialists for each project.

In Numbers

International experience, a broader role

International trade experience provides a foundation of relationships. We are developing a broader integration model around technology selection, local needs, collaboration and sustainability, bringing in specialist capabilities as each initiative requires.

For Whom

Turn an infrastructure need into a clearer brief, with suitable solutions and partners. Understand the project proposition, its participants and the questions to assess before investing. Connect your technology with potential applications, local partners and international cooperation.

  • See it before you decide

    The proposed Vienna centre would bring together building, energy, water and other sustainable solutions for demonstrations and international cooperation. Its scope, partnerships and funding remain under development.

  • Solutions matched to local needs

    We help clarify the brief, compare relevant approaches and identify technology providers, engineers and local partners. Specialist teams assess suitability for local climate, standards, operation and maintenance.

  • Preparing the funding conversation

    We help organise project information and discussions with potential funders. Qualified advisers would prepare technical studies, environmental assessments and financing documentation where required. Funding remains subject to assessment and approval.

  • Clear requirements and responsibilities

    We help identify documentation and procurement requirements with clients and specialist partners. Suppliers and advisers provide the relevant product evidence, assessments and certifications for the selected solutions.

Request a project brief
  • A clearer project proposition

    We help bring the need, proposed solutions, participants and open questions into a shared project brief. Potential investors then have a clearer basis for deciding whether to commission further assessment.

  • Identify information gaps early

    With relevant specialists, we help identify technical, environmental and local requirements that need investigation. Due diligence and compliance assessments remain with the appointed professionals.

  • One point of coordination

    We coordinate dialogue between participants, help clarify roles and track agreed next steps. Each specialist retains responsibility for its own work, contracts and professional decisions.

  • Relevant partners for each initiative

    Our international trade experience is a starting point for partner identification. Additional manufacturers, engineers, research organisations and local specialists are approached according to the needs of each initiative.

Discuss co-investment
  • Open to suitable technology partners

    We welcome dialogue with providers of building, energy, water and other sustainable technologies. Potential participation depends on project requirements, technical assessment and agreed responsibilities and terms.

  • Develop international cooperation

    We help explore opportunities and introduce potential clients, local partners and advisers. Market entry, procurement and approvals require project-specific work with the relevant authorities and specialists.

  • Shape a shared project brief

    We help turn initial interest into a clearer brief: the need, possible applications, participants and questions to resolve. Partners can then assess whether and how to contribute.

  • Visibility with decision-makers

    The proposed Vienna centre is intended for technology demonstrations, professional exchange and meetings with potential project partners. Participation and the operating model remain under development.
