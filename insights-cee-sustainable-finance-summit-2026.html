Finance for infrastructure that lasts

We took part in the CEE Sustainable Finance Summit 2026 in Prague to discuss how sustainable finance can support low-carbon infrastructure in the region.

The summit, organised by the International Sustainable Finance Centre, brought selected senior representatives from business, finance and policymaking to Prague from 14 to 16 September. Its three days moved from geopolitics, finance and AI, through business and electrification, to critical infrastructure and resilience.

Infrastructure that lasts needs finance that is patient and clear about its goals. Conversations like these help us understand what funders look for, so that project initiatives can identify the information and specialist assessments needed for a funding discussion.
