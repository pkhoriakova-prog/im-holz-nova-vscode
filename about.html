The Shift

From timber trade to integrated timber infrastructure

Wood has always been at the core of what we do — a renewable material that connects the built environment with nature. Building on two decades of experience in international timber markets, we have moved beyond supplying material to understanding what it can enable: better infrastructure that is resource-efficient, adaptable and designed to work with nature.

Our Vision

Building with wood, beyond forest borders

Wood is becoming the defining building material of our century. We bring certified European timber and Austrian engineering to regions where timber construction has so far been out of reach, laying the foundations for healthy, low-carbon places where the next generation can learn, heal and live.

Four Pillars

How we build

Our path to becoming an integrator of sustainable infrastructure rests on four pillars: European materials and Austrian engineering, local people and culture, collaboration, and sustainability.

  • European Materials & Austrian Engineering

    Certified engineered timber (CLT, glulam, structural solid wood) from responsibly managed forests, produced to EU standards. Behind every project stands Austrian know-how in timber design and fire safety.

  • Local People & Culture

    We build with the communities we serve, not just for them. Local architects, builders and institutions are involved from the start. We share knowledge and skills so that timber construction can take root locally, and we design for regional climate, traditions and ways of life.

  • Collaboration

    No single company delivers sustainable infrastructure alone. For each project we bring together manufacturers, engineers, research institutions, public authorities and development finance, and we build relationships that last beyond a single contract.

    See the ecosystem

  • Sustainability

    For us, sustainability is measurable: lower embodied carbon, healthier indoor spaces, resource-efficient construction and buildings that stay socially useful for decades. Our projects are designed to align with recognised frameworks such as EN 15978, the EU Taxonomy and the UN SDGs.
